Holiday Outboards

Coming from a background of racing outboard-powered flat watercraft, we supply and service a wide range of outboards, specializing in the Bombardier, Johnson, and Evinrude ranges.

Here at Holiday Outboards, we carry a full range of parts and accessories for most outboard makes and have all the specialist equipment and factory-trained personnel to carry out any service or repair job. We offer a free price estimate service on larger jobs and can store your whole boat under cover if need be.

We have a large Boat-Park facility adjoining our workshop for inexpensive boat parking. Drop-in some time and have a look at the many qualification certificates proudly displayed on our showroom walls and you will be assured of a fast economical friendly service, or drop us an e-mail. We look forward to meeting you in person soon.

Holiday Outboard Celebrates 50 Years

Celebrating 50 Years of Services & Boat Storage.

Published 19 July 2024

For over 50 years, Holiday Outboard Services has been dedicated to servicing and repairing outboard motors of all makes and models, specialising as Johnson, Evinrude, and Honda dealers. The company’s journey began in 1968 when Keith Smith founded Holiday Marine. In 1975, Ian Gallop took the helm, and in 1989, current owner Stuart Andrew joined the team. The company was rebranded to Holiday Outboard Services in 1991, the name under which it operates today.

Holiday Outboard Services caters to a diverse clientele from all over New Zealand and the Pacific Islands. Many customers have been loyal for years, returning repeatedly for the exceptional service and respect they receive. The team of Stu (38), Jeremy (38), Lance (25) and John (5) — years of experience — is known for taking the time to explain issues and provide effective solutions, boasting one of the largest selections of parts for older engines in Auckland. With decades of accumulated knowledge and experience, Holiday Outboard Services is truly a customer-focused business.

The pride in their work is evident when talking to the team at Holiday Outboards, with their commitment driven by three main factors:

Safety Ensuring the safety of boat owners, their families, and friends is a top priority. As Stuart Andrew says, “It can get very nasty very fast on the water.”
Expertise The staff are highly trained, with Master Technicians on site. Collectively, the staff boasts over 106 years of experience in the industry.
Dedication The team are passionate about providing the best service possible because they themselves are boaties and genuinely love what they do. Stuart Andrew, the Director, has over 38 years of experience in the marine industry, including offshore power-boat and circuit racing. Before his career in marine, Stuart served in the UK military before emigrating to New Zealand.

In addition to their service and repair shop, Holiday Outboards also operates a 4,000-square-metre, fully secured storage facility on-site. The facility offers 24-hour access via individually coded security tags, boat wash facilities, a drop-off and pick-up service to boat ramps, and a boat valet service. For more information, call (09) 448 1650 or visit them at 209 Bush Road, Rosedale, Auckland.
